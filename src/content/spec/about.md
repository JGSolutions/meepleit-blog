# About

Welcome! I'm Jerry Gagliano, and I have a passion for medium to heavy strategy games.

I discovered this hobby in January 2023. I was always eager to play Risk, and when I finally did, I was fascinated by the game's mechanics. Initially, I thought popular games were limited to Monopoly, Risk, and Dungeons & Dragons. However, after receiving recommendations for games similar to Risk, I realized the vastness of the board game industry. I spent hours on YouTube and discovered the existence of BoardGameGeek.

Fast forward to now, and I've played many games, including Terraforming Mars, Brass, Imperium Horizons/Classics/Legends, Robinson Crusoe, Anachrony, Scythe, and many more.

The goal of this blog is not to provide typical game reviews. I usually replay games to understand their full scope. I want to share my experiences with different strategies and discuss the thematic depth and replayability of various games.

I hope you find my blogs enjoyable and, most importantly, helpful.

Game on!
