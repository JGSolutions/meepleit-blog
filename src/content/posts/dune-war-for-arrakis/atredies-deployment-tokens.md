I been playing with the Artiedies and having the prvilge of learning the dept strategic for this faction. Have I been successful...NO! But I have to admit I am learning how to play with the Atredies. If you like playing with factions that have objective goals for this is definitely for you. It's also not only about completing the objectives but to defend from the Harkoneens as they will come and attack your scetiches.

One small detail rule that my friend mentioned was having a free action to reveal any number of deployment tokens. At the sametime we were asking ourseleves "Why would we want to reveal any deployment tokens?" Isn't it best to keep it hidden until the Harkonens come and attack me with out knowning what kind of battle there heading into? Well maybe not! Here are some ideas why you should take advantage to reveal them.

## Helping Complete Your Objectives

The Atredies hang out in the outskirts of the dessert which there scites are surrounding the Harkkonen's settlements. They have special dessert abilities actions like sanding riding and placing sandworms. So they can move around the dessert more quickly. There strategy are not to go agressive on the Harkoneens but have a set of objective cards to accomplish every round which will level them up the preseance track.

The problem is you can just leave your scitches opened in the wild as the Harkkoenes can just come an attack and gain there level track. One way is reveal the deplymeent tokens and have certain units run off in the dessert to complete objectives if possible

## Make Your Sietches Stronger

The Atredies have a limit of actions compared to the Harkonnens. If you get lucky and roll some deployment actions, take advantage of them and deploy them to your sietches. Your sietch unit will be stronger and larger as you will be able to easily combat against the Harkonnens. Remember the Harkonnens can upgrade their units that can make it harder to beat them. So deploy those units, plant the seeds and make them grow.

## Consqueneces

Be careful not to go 0 crazy and deployment them all over as you do have a limit. When removing a deplyment token it's completely removed from the game and can not be reused.
