I've had the privilege of playing Dune: War for Arrakis weekly, focusing on mastering the Atreides faction. Despite diving into some deep strategic ideas, success has been elusive. Still, I love the challenge and continue to experiment with new strategies to improve my gameplay.

If you enjoy playing asymmetric factions with clear objective goals, this game is a great fit. It's not just about achieving objectives, but also about defending against the Harkonnens, who will relentlessly attack your settlements.

A small detail that caught our attention was the Atreides' ability to take a free action to reveal any number of deployment tokens. We were puzzled—why reveal them early? Wouldn't it be more strategic to keep them hidden until the Harkonnens attack, leaving them uncertain about what kind of battle they're walking into?

Maybe not! Here are some strategic reasons why revealing your deployment tokens can actually be advantageous.

## Claim Your Objectives

The Atredies sietches are out in the outskirts of the dessert protecting their sietches which are surrounding the Harkonnens's settlements. They have special dessert abilities action like sandriding which is a key mechanic that allows the Atreides, to move swiftly across the desert. This ability represents their mastery of the desert environment, giving them a significant tactical advantage by enabling fast and unpredictable movement.

By revealing your deployment tokens, you will have the ability for some of your units sandride and work on claiming the objectves as the other units can stay back on protecting the sietches. The problem is you can't just leave the sietches alonne opened in the wild as the Harkonnens can just attack which will help them gain points on their level track. Then once you claim an objective you quickly swiftly move your units to bring them back to the base.

In my situation, I had some of my units have not moved from the sietches. They stayed out in the opening enjoying the sand as my other sietches were being attack. I didn't know what to do as I was worried to move my units away from the sietches. Also by being attacked, I never had enough actions to deploy more tokens. I wasn't playing it correct and didn't know what to do in those sitautions. Now knowing that a free action is available to reveal my deployment tokens, I could have taken advantage.

I'm not suggesting you reveal all your tokens across the board, but in certain situations, it can be strategic. If you need to move units out into the desert to secure objectives, that’s when deploying additional tokens and revealing your units can be beneficial. By doing so, you can reinforce key areas, prepare for battles, or create a tactical advantage where it's most needed. The goal isn't just to expose your forces, but to use their positioning wisely to gain the upper hand.

Some of the objectives require you to sandride to cliam the objectves. Some require to have some uunits alone in the dessert. One objective requires you to destory 2 harvesters on one turn to claim those objectives. This is when you need too be smart to reveal your tokens.

Another suggestion, don't have the Atredies go agressively and attack head on with the Harkonnens but instead focus on capturing objectives points within the round which will level you up the preseance track. Especially at the start of the game this important to start planning & tackling those objectives as the Harkonnens units will be stronger as the game progesses which eventually can be difficult to win the battles and slow you down on claiming the objectives.

## Consquences

Be careful, don't go crazy deploying them all over the board as you do have a limit. You will eventually run out of units and your deployment action dice no rrlevant and wastes. Deploy your token (and leader of course)when needed and accomplish your objectives.
