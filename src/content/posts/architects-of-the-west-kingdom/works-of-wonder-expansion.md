Works of Wonder is the second expansion for the Architects of the West Kingdom as it brings five glorious momuments to beautify the city. But only architects with high reputation will earn this great honor.

The expansion introduces more components, rules and enhances the base game by adding depth, more strategic options, and additional paths to victory. We'll dive into the new mechanics, features, and how they enhance the base game, helping you decide if it's a must-have for your collection.

Let's get started!

## New Mechanics & Theme

!A Picture of the 5 Wonders

The king is expanding the town and adding 5 new structure wonders. Each wonder is tied with a resource: clay, gold, marble, wood and mines.

!A Picture of the new board

The expansion introduces the new influence track board which matches perfectly and placed next to the main board. The influence track allows to build one of the 5 wonders which is an extra cost on top of the tax and reources. The track can also provide you tax money and scoring benefits.

!Show picture of Princess and Profiteer
For the architects to built these wonders, they need to accompany the Princess. When placing a worker on the Princess's location, they immediality contribute a resource with addition of their influence increasing. Similar to the Virtue track.
The Princess is clearly a kind-hearted character, but if you do something mischievous, like capturing workers in her location, she'll penalize you on the virtue track.

The Profiteer that represents someone who takes advantage of situations for personal gain, often at the expense of others. He provides you an influence only when placing a worker on his location.  If you capture workers at his location, he'll reward you with a virtue for your mischievous actions. Sounds great! But when the black market resets depending on the Consquence card, you will have to pay for it. So be careful not to hang out with the Profiteer.

## Lets Beautify the Town

!Picture of the wonders
Now your wondering, how do we build that wonder? What's the advantage?

Collecting resources is still important, with the influence and coins. Gathering a large amount resources as some of these wonders require up to 15 resources. Check the photo of the wonders, on the top left you will see the number of resources required.

Why even bother? Just got for the catherdrale or buildings?

Well by gaining a structure, it will provide you the options to place the structure in any location. When you place a worker onto your structure's location you have two options: count the worker as 2 workers or the gain an influence. Depends on the strategy you aiming for. If you need gold, you'll place the structure on the Mine location helping you gather resources for other buildings. You will be creating some kind of engine producing resources.

Here is the strategy! Try collecting resources and aim for a wonder. Then place that structure on another location and aim for those resources. On that of that, if your lucky, the Profiteer is on the same location as your structue you will increase an influence at the same time.
