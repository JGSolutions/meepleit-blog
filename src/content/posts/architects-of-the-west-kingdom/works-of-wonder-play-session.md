The king has spoken and as we get ready to build those wonders to impress our king. Charles gets ready to play against the bot Illusionist.

To start, my architect(Charles) has two wood resources as I decided to get a head start for the Wonder. My influence began at the bottom at level two which I needed to work on my reputation. Contributing reources with the Princess is always a great help as your influence track will increase for each resource that has been contributed

Of course AI sending workers at mulitpule locations like the kingstore which helps them increases their virtue. For some reason they fleew up on the virtue track. I always kept an eye on their workers and capturing them.

Constructing the Wood wonder was a challenge as I had to manage the AI's overzealous workers while simultaneously building my influence. Once completed, I strategically placed it on the mine location to target either the gold or mines wonder. Unfortunately, the AI claimed the gold wonder, forcing me to pivot my strategy and focus on acquiring the fifteen mine resources required for the mine wonder.

The AI was realy setting thre workers in mulitpule locations. The tax stand was increasing with a pile of coins which I did attack it a few times and collected a nice chunk of change.

In meantime I lost track of getting the mine wonders. I did advance on the catherdrale as it was a race between me and the bot as we were placing our workers on the Guildhall and heading towards the end of game. I eventually did manage to get the mine wonder but the bot was quick and built the clay and marble wonder structures.

Think of it, I liked going after the wonder at the start of the game but a lot of resources and time needed to build those wonders just like in real life. I set all my eggs in one basket. I rarely hire apprentices, just enough to build the buildings that I want. Inn this case I ended up building only one building

The final result was 48 - 43 for the AI bot. It was a tight game as I did play some defensive strateegies to avoid the AI to accumulate extra points but lost focus and certain areas like collecting resources.
