I previous wrote a blog on [Undaunted: Normandy vs North Africa](https://meepleit.com/boardgame/367150/dune-war-for-arrakis) which I gave an overview of the differences between both games which this expansion adds more content for the games.

I've always found the Undaunted series intriguing, but I completely overlooked these games due to the lack of a solo mode. I never really explored the series in depth, so discovering this expansion was a bit of a challenge for me.

I got lucky with one board gamer friend I met through the facebook community asking me if I ever touched any of Undaunted series. Replied no right away. He was surprised because, as a solo player, the game featured an excellent solo mode within the Reinforcements expansion.

I was shocked to learn that the series actually had a solo mode. He was kind enough to send me his copy of the game along with Normandy, and it turned out to be an amazing gift and a perfect introduction to the series.

## Overview

Undaunted series can be confusing at  first as there are so many of them. This is only an expanion adding more content for the Normandy and North Africa game. Both games are not needed but you will obiviously need one of them.

The expansion introduces a range of new rules, scenarios, and units. Take on either the Germans or American as tanks are introduced in Undaunted: Normandy, or make use of the mines, assault aircraft, and other new units as you attempt to beat your opponent in Undaunted: North Africa.
