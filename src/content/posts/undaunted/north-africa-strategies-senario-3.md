In January 11, 1941, the Italians land in South-westen Libya as the LRDG's objective is to prevent the Italian from garrison the airfield in Undaunted North Africa

I will be playing the Italians in Scenario 3, as we need to neutralize the LRDG Sergeant and Staff Sergeant to gain the airfield and need too claim 1 objective point.

Scenario 3 is not a complex nor tough as not a lot of units are set in the game setup but introduces vehicles as combat will be bit harder and more tatical strategies involved. 

The LRDG have an advantage at the start of the game as they are already placed in vehicles with with more armary. The Italians are on foot but need to race to the Scout car in the desert. Same time some Italians units need to stay behind and protect the objective point as the LRDG can come storming in.

Off the start I use the Scout to start scouting and moving towards the empty Scout car. The idea is to jump in and go pick up some on unit that can provide some extra combat benefits. What is interesting with the vehicles is you can transport units around more quickly with extra protection along the way. Nonethless need to be carefull as the LRDG anti-gunners can fire
